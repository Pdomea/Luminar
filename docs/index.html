Precision Engineering

Quiet technology that respects your flow.

Every surface and state transition in Luminar is designed to feel effortless—staying invisible while you create and guiding your recovery when your eyes need relief.

01 / STEALTH HUD

Minimalist Floating Timepiece

Encapsulated inside a dedicated Shadow DOM root so it renders with identical typography and frosted-glass clarity across every website. Reposition it freely or toggle circadian filtration with a single tap.

02 / MEETING GUARD

Intelligent Call Awareness

Automatically recognizes active sessions on Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Webex, and fullscreen presentations—gracefully deferring recovery breaks until your conversation concludes.

03 / STRUCTURED RECOVERY

Biometric Ocular Cadence

Each 60-second recovery cycle transitions smoothly from far-point ciliary decompression to dynamic smooth pursuit and deep tear-film blink restoration, complemented by a 90-second deep reset every fourth session.

04 / CIRCADIAN FILTRATION

Adaptive Warmth Layer

Applies a zero-latency optical multiply filter at document start across three calibrated warmth intensities (Low, Mid, High) to reduce harsh short-wavelength glare during late sessions.
