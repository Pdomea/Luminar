Privacy Policy — Luminar

Effective Date: November 2026  ·  Author: Dominik (Pdomea)

01 / Zero Data Collection

Luminar operates strictly locally inside your browser. We do not collect, log, store on external servers, sell, or transmit any personal information, browsing history, page content, or user analytics.

02 / Local Browser Storage

Luminar uses Chrome's native chrome.storage API exclusively to persist your timer preferences and session statistics on your own device:

  • Active timer state & countdown timestamp (luminar_engine_state, luminar_engine_endTime)
  • Selected focus duration in minutes (luminar_work_duration_minutes)
  • Completed ocular recovery counter (luminar_pause_count)
  • Blue-light filter state & warmth level (luminar_bluelight_active, luminar_bluelight_intensity)
  • Meeting guard status (luminar_meeting_mode_active)

03 / Permissions

  • storage: Persists your focus timer settings and completed session count locally.
  • alarms: Triggers deterministic background focus-to-recovery transitions in Manifest V3 service workers.
  • content_scripts (<all_urls>): Renders the draggable Stealth HUD, the warm Blue-Light Filter overlay, and the full-screen Ocular Recovery sequences, and locally checks if the active hostname is a video call (Meet, Zoom, Teams, Webex) so breaks never interrupt meetings.
